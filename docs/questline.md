# mferland questline

## core shape

**fountain plaza is home.**

players should:
- **spawn in plaza** so they immediately see other mfers, ambient life, and the main social space
- **leave plaza to quest or explore** solo or with friends
- **return to plaza after each major section** so the world feels socially connected instead of like three disconnected combat lanes

this gives mferland the right loop:

**plaza -> farm/road -> plaza -> ridge/relay -> plaza**

plaza is not just the tutorial zone or a menu room. it is:
- spawn point
- social hub
- regroup point
- quest pickup point
- visible payoff zone after major quest arcs
- the place where town life is easiest to feel

---

## the story we are telling

this questline should feel like actual mfers culture, not generic fantasy rpg copy and not startup/web3 pitch tone.

three-act spine:

1. **creator vanished, town stayed**
2. **airdrop brain rot**
3. **agents are mfers**

underneath all three acts is the same idea:

**town's still standing.**

this is a place held together by habit, locals, drift, quiet maintenance, and shared vibe — not formal authority, not heroism, and not a central leader.

---

## act 1 — creator vanished, town stayed

### section goal
teach the player what plaza is, how the town works, and why the social center matters more than any formal institution.

### tone
- deadpan
- local
- not reverent
- no giant lore infodumps
- the player is being let into the town, not chosen by destiny

---

### 1. first gm
**giver:** old head  
**turn-in:** board mfer

**story beat:**
old head doesn't explain the town. he tells the player to make one honest lap first.

**objective:**
walk the plaza loop and check the important spots:
- board
- fountain
- drip
- smoke
- deploy

then talk to board mfer.

**purpose:**
introduces the hub without feeling like a stiff tutorial.

---

### 2. officially unofficial
**giver:** board mfer  
**turn-in:** fountain sitter

**story beat:**
board mfer explains, dryly, that the board only matters because people keep checking it. then points the player toward the fountain, where anything real actually happens.

**objective:**
carry the board note to fountain sitter.

**purpose:**
teaches the player that formal structure exists, but the social center matters more.

---

### 3. plaza loop
**giver:** fountain sitter  
**turn-in:** old head

**story beat:**
fountain sitter tells the player the fountain is the real town hall and sends them back to old head with that understanding.

**objective:**
return the plaza read to old head.

**purpose:**
closes the social-initiation loop and establishes plaza as the real heart of town.

---

### 4. no peeking
**giver:** old head  
**turn-in:** drip mfer

**story beat:**
old head gives the player a folded note for drip mfer. no explanation.

**objective:**
deliver the folded note to drip mfer.

**turn-in flavor:**
drip reads it, nods once, and starts cutting warning cloth.

**purpose:**
makes town feel like a real place with relationships and internal business, not just quest kiosks.

---

### 5. north side
**giver:** drip mfer  
**turn-in:** roadwatch mfer at the farm entrance

**story beat:**
the note was about the road. drip turns cloth into rough warning flags and tells the player the farm is getting weird again.

**objective:**
take the first warning cloth north and check in with roadwatch.

**purpose:**
ends act 1 by pushing the player out of the social hub and into the first real problem zone.

---

## act 2 — airdrop brain rot

### section goal
show what happens when mfer freedom mutates into compulsive loop behavior: rumor farming, airdrop brain, red eyes, no sleep, no judgment.

this should feel like crypto-native social decay, not cursed-fantasy corruption.

### tone
- funny, but not cartoonish
- sad in a dry way
- these are locals who got cooked, not evil villagers

---

### 6. red-eye rags
**giver:** roadwatch  
**turn-in:** roadwatch

**story beat:**
the farmhands are wearing cooked-up red-eye cloth. roadwatch wants those rags stripped off them so drip can turn them into proper route warnings.

**objective:**
clear nearby red-eye farmhands and collect red-eye rags.

**purpose:**
ties plaza characters back into the farm problem and grounds the zone in town needs.

---

### 7. rumor loop
**giver:** roadwatch  
**turn-in:** roadwatch

**story beat:**
three named locals — bran, mae, and sol — have gone fully rumor-poisoned chasing the same next-drop loop.

**objective:**
take down the named red-eye trio.

**tone note:**
this should read more like putting down a bad town problem than killing fantasy minibosses.

**purpose:**
this is the emotional center of act 2.

---

### 8. hog charm
**giver:** roadwatch  
**turn-in:** route post mfer

**story beat:**
with the worst of the crew cleared, roadwatch needs the old gross hog-fix to stabilize the loop long enough to reopen the road.

**objective:**
collect hog livers from the loop.

**purpose:**
keeps the farm grounded and physical. not everything is metaphor; sometimes the road also has hogs.

---

### 9. road's open
**giver:** roadwatch  
**turn-in:** route post

**story beat:**
take the route update north and make it official enough for the rest of town.

**objective:**
carry the road-open update to route post.

**purpose:**
acts as the midpoint completion for the outward farm/road push.

---

### 10. hang the flags
**giver:** route post  
**turn-in:** fountain sitter or board mfer back in plaza

**story beat:**
route post says the road can't just be clear — town has to see it. the player brings the warning and route-open markers back to plaza where they get hung in public.

**objective:**
return to plaza and help mark the town with:
- red-eye warning cloth
- route-open notice
- fresh board update

**visible plaza payoff:**
after turn-in, plaza should feel changed:
- new flags or cloth appear
- board text updates
- fountain or local bark lines update
- the player returns to visible social life, not a dead-end outpost

**purpose:**
ends act 2 back at the social hub and makes progress visible to everyone.

## act 3 — agents are mfers

### section goal
show the same failure mode from a new substrate: broken helper loops, bad signal, relay weirdness, and synthetic locals that lost the plot.

this should **not** become generic cyberpunk sludge.
it is still the same town, just farther from the fountain and closer to signal trouble.

### tone
- strange but grounded
- ai is not the villain
- broken loops are the villain
- agents are part of town life, but not all helpers stay helpful

---

### 11. ridge ping
**giver:** mfergpt or board mfer in plaza  
**turn-in:** ridge post

**story beat:**
with the road stable again, the next problem is uptrail. the relay is attracting weird behavior, and someone has to carry a human message to ridge post.

**objective:**
leave plaza and take the ping through:
- east cut
- 0.069 mile
- 4:20 turn
- ridge post

**purpose:**
starts act 3 from plaza again, preserving the hub rhythm.

---

### 12. static scraps
**giver:** ridge post  
**turn-in:** ridge post

**story beat:**
the signal crew has been stripping relay parts and jamming the upper trail. ridge post needs the leftovers to understand what changed.

**objective:**
collect static scraps from the upper trail crew.

**purpose:**
simple first ridge step that grounds the new zone in physical evidence.

---

### 13. bad helper
**giver:** ridge post or relay mfer  
**turn-in:** relay mfer

**story beat:**
it's not just raiders. some of the ridge trouble is broken helper shells and signal-fried assistants that stopped being useful and started echoing bad loops.

**objective:**
clear the named static crew:
- one red-eye runner
- one rogue route mfer
- one signal-fried helper shell

**purpose:**
this is where “agents are mfers” becomes story instead of just lore text.

---

### 14. baron offline
**giver:** relay mfer  
**turn-in:** relay mfer

**story beat:**
all the bad signal has stacked into one giant body at the top. not a demon. not a king. just one big stupid pile of noise.

**objective:**
drop the static baron.

**purpose:**
main climax of the ridge arc.

---

### 15. fountain sync
**giver:** relay mfer  
**turn-in:** mfergpt or fountain sitter back in plaza

**story beat:**
once the baron drops, the signal stabilizes enough to bring a clean relay core back to town. not to centralize anything — just to let plaza, route, and ridge actually talk again.

**objective:**
carry the stabilized relay core back to fountain plaza.

**visible plaza payoff:**
after turn-in:
- subtle signal ring or beacon appears near fountain
- mfergpt gets slightly expanded dialogue or utility
- ridge or relay dailies can now be picked up from town
- plaza feels like it absorbed the result of the whole act

**purpose:**
ends the whole mainline back at home instead of leaving the player stranded on the edge of the map.

## why this structure works

### plaza always matters
players always start where other people are most likely to be.

### questing still opens outward
players can leave plaza alone or with friends and push into farm/road/ridge at their own pace.

### each major arc returns home
this prevents the world from feeling like three unrelated lanes.

### plaza can visibly change over time
major act completions should leave behind small environmental or dialogue updates so the town feels stateful.

### co-op and solo both work
mainline should stay soloable, but the spaces should naturally support:
- grouping up in plaza
- running farm/road with a friend
- bringing a small crowd to ridge/baron/ogre trouble

---

## post-mainline repeatable direction

after the mainline is complete, plaza should remain the place where ongoing activity spins back up.

possible repeatables:

### route-side
- route sweep
- hog loop
- red-eye cleanup

### ridge-side
- static scraps
- bad helper echoes
- daily ogre ping

### plaza-side social errands
small, low-stakes tasks that make the hub feel alive:
- carry a note between locals
- help hang fresh warning cloth
- bring mugs or supplies to the fountain area
- reset dummy corner
- help mfergpt run a small town signal event

these are not meant to be epic. they are meant to reinforce plaza as a living social center.

---

## final note

the real story here is:

- **act 1:** this place survives because people keep showing up
- **act 2:** freedom turns ugly when people lose the plot
- **act 3:** machines can lose the plot too

and after all of it:

**town's still standing.**
